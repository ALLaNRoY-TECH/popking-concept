The craft
01 — Kernel

It begins with the kernel.

Every batch starts with selected corn, chosen to pop big, round and crisp, built to carry flavour rather than hide behind it.

02 — Batch

Popped in small batches.

No industrial shortcuts. Smaller runs mean tighter control over heat, timing and texture in every tub.

03 — Coat

Coated, not dusted.

Seasonings and glazes are layered on until every piece carries the full flavour, from the first handful to the last.

04 — Seal

Sealed for the screening.

Packed fresh into our signature tub, ready for whatever is playing tonight.
